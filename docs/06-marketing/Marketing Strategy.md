# FixNest Marketing Strategy v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how FixNest should attract, convert, retain, and grow customers while building trust as Pakistan's home ecosystem platform.

## 2. Marketing Objectives

- Generate qualified service bookings.
- Build brand trust in target service areas.
- Educate homeowners about maintenance and safety.
- Create repeat usage across multiple service categories.
- Recruit reliable technicians.
- Prepare the market for future memberships, marketplace, and home management features.

## 3. Positioning

FixNest should position itself as a trusted home ecosystem, not only a repair marketplace. The core message is that customers get verified professionals, transparent processes, reliable support, and service history through one platform.

## 4. Target Segments

### 4.1 Urban Homeowners

Need reliable help for repairs, maintenance, upgrades, and emergency issues.

### 4.2 Tenants

Need quick repair support and may influence landlords or property managers.

### 4.3 Working Professionals

Value convenience, scheduled visits, transparent pricing, and trusted technicians.

### 4.4 Families

Value safety, verified workers, predictable service, and support after the job.

### 4.5 Property Managers and Small Offices

Need recurring service support, documentation, and dependable response times.

## 5. Brand Messages

Primary messages:

- Verified home service professionals.
- Transparent pricing guidance.
- One platform for home repairs and maintenance.
- Customer support and complaint handling.
- Service history and future maintenance reminders.

## 6. Launch Channels

### 6.1 Local SEO

Create service pages and content for target cities and service categories.

### 6.2 Google Search Ads

Target high-intent keywords such as electrician near me, plumber near me, AC service, leakage repair, and fan installation.

### 6.3 Social Media

Use Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts for trust-building content, before-and-after jobs, maintenance tips, and seasonal campaigns.

### 6.4 WhatsApp

Use WhatsApp for booking follow-up, reminders, referral sharing, and customer support.

### 6.5 Referral Program

Reward customers for referring friends, family, neighbors, and housing society groups.

### 6.6 Partnerships

Build relationships with housing societies, property dealers, appliance shops, hardware stores, electricians, plumbers, AC part sellers, and facility managers.

## 7. Content Strategy

Content should focus on:

- Electrical safety.
- Plumbing leakage prevention.
- AC seasonal maintenance.
- Home maintenance checklists.
- Cost-saving tips.
- Buying guides for home products.
- Warning signs that require professional help.

Content should educate first and sell second.

## 8. Campaign Ideas

- Summer AC service campaign.
- Monsoon leakage prevention campaign.
- Electrical safety week.
- New customer first-service offer.
- Refer a neighbor campaign.
- Home maintenance checklist download.
- Society partnership discount.

## 9. Customer Retention

Retention tactics:

- Post-service follow-up.
- Maintenance reminders.
- Repeat customer discounts.
- Seasonal service reminders.
- Service history records.
- Annual Maintenance Contract offers.
- Helpful WhatsApp tips.

## 10. Technician Recruitment Marketing

Technician messaging should emphasize:

- More job opportunities.
- Fair payment process.
- Reputation building.
- Training and professional growth.
- Structured customer support.

Recruitment channels may include referrals, local markets, trade groups, Facebook groups, technical institutes, and supplier shops.

## 11. Marketing Funnel

### Awareness

SEO, social media, local partnerships, educational content, and community groups.

### Consideration

Service pages, reviews, pricing guidance, trust content, and FAQs.

### Conversion

Booking form, WhatsApp CTA, phone support, first-service offers, and fast response.

### Retention

Follow-ups, reminders, loyalty offers, and maintenance plans.

### Advocacy

Referral program, testimonials, reviews, and society group sharing.

## 12. Budget Allocation

During launch, spend conservatively and measure every channel. Suggested allocation:

- 35% search ads.
- 20% social ads.
- 15% content and SEO.
- 15% referral and loyalty offers.
- 10% partnerships and local activations.
- 5% testing new channels.

## 13. Key Metrics

- Leads by channel.
- Booking conversion rate.
- Cost per lead.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Repeat booking rate.
- Referral rate.
- Review score.
- Website conversion rate.
- WhatsApp click-to-book rate.
- Revenue by campaign.

## 14. Launch Plan

### Month 1

Finalize website, booking flow, local SEO pages, social profiles, and initial paid search tests.

### Month 2

Launch referral program, publish weekly content, test social ads, and approach housing societies.

### Month 3

Optimize campaigns based on completed jobs, expand best-performing services, collect testimonials, and launch seasonal offers.

## 15. Marketing Governance

All marketing claims must match operational capability. FixNest should not advertise services, response times, prices, warranties, or service areas that operations cannot reliably support.
