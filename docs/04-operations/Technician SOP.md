# FixNest Technician SOPs v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the behavior, safety, service quality, communication, and reporting standards expected from every FixNest technician.

## 2. Technician Code of Conduct

Technicians must:

- Be respectful, punctual, and professional.
- Carry or display FixNest identification where available.
- Work only in verified skill categories.
- Never mislead customers about pricing, parts, or service outcomes.
- Never demand unauthorized extra payment.
- Never share customer personal information.
- Never behave in a way that damages customer trust or FixNest reputation.

## 3. Assignment Acceptance

Before accepting a job, technicians must check:

- Service category.
- Customer area.
- Preferred time.
- Issue description.
- Required tools.
- Personal availability.
- Whether the job is within verified skill scope.

If a technician cannot arrive on time or cannot perform the job safely, they must decline or inform operations immediately.

## 4. Preparation Before Visit

Technicians should prepare:

- Required tools.
- Safety equipment.
- Basic consumables.
- Phone with sufficient battery.
- Customer address and booking details.
- FixNest support contact.

Technicians must inform operations before departure and provide realistic arrival estimates.

## 5. Arrival Standard

On arrival, technicians must:

- Greet the customer politely.
- Confirm booking details.
- Introduce themselves as assigned by FixNest.
- Request permission before entering specific rooms or work areas.
- Respect household rules and privacy.

## 6. Inspection Standard

Technicians must inspect the issue before quoting final pricing for non-standard jobs. They should explain:

- What appears to be wrong.
- What work is recommended.
- Whether parts or materials are needed.
- Estimated time required.
- Risks, limitations, or warranty conditions.

Technicians must avoid exaggerating problems or recommending unnecessary work.

## 7. Pricing and Approval

Technicians must:

- Follow FixNest pricing guidance.
- Communicate labor and material charges clearly.
- Obtain customer approval before starting chargeable work.
- Inform operations about unusual or high-value quotes.
- Never add hidden charges after work is complete.

## 8. Work Quality Standard

Technicians must:

- Use appropriate tools and methods.
- Follow safety practices.
- Test work before declaring completion.
- Avoid shortcuts that create future risk.
- Protect customer property from avoidable damage.
- Clean the immediate work area after completion.

## 9. Safety Requirements

Technicians must stop work and call operations if:

- The job is outside their skill scope.
- The location is unsafe.
- Electrical wiring, gas, water pressure, or structural conditions create risk.
- The customer requests illegal, unsafe, or unethical work.
- A disagreement with the customer becomes heated.

## 10. Communication Rules

Technicians must:

- Update operations when en route, arrived, started work, shared quote, completed work, collected payment, or faced an issue.
- Inform customers and operations about delays.
- Use polite language.
- Avoid arguments.
- Route disputes through FixNest support.

## 11. Payment Handling

Technicians may collect payment only through approved methods. They must:

- Record amount collected.
- Separate labor, material, and visit charges where required.
- Share invoice or receipt information.
- Submit cash reconciliation as instructed.
- Never collect personal tips as a condition for service.

## 12. Evidence and Documentation

Technicians should provide:

- Arrival confirmation.
- Inspection notes.
- Before photos where appropriate.
- Quotation details.
- Customer approval record.
- Completion notes.
- After photos where appropriate.
- Parts used.
- Payment status.

## 13. Customer Property Rules

Technicians must not:

- Use customer belongings without permission.
- Move furniture unnecessarily.
- Leave waste behind.
- Damage walls, tiles, appliances, or fittings without prior explanation and approval.
- Bring unapproved helpers to the customer location.

## 14. Warranty and Revisit Rules

Technicians must honor FixNest warranty rules where applicable. If a customer reports a repeat issue:

- Do not blame the customer without evidence.
- Inspect objectively.
- Report findings to operations.
- Complete approved warranty rework professionally.

## 15. Performance Standards

Technician performance will be measured by:

- Punctuality.
- Completion rate.
- Customer rating.
- Complaint rate.
- Rework rate.
- Pricing compliance.
- Documentation quality.
- Professional behavior.

## 16. Disciplinary Actions

FixNest may apply coaching, warning, temporary suspension, category restriction, payment hold for investigation, or removal from the platform for:

- Fraudulent charging.
- Repeated poor ratings.
- Unsafe work.
- Harassment or disrespect.
- Property damage.
- Customer data misuse.
- Job abandonment.
- Unauthorized subcontracting.

## 17. Technician Support

FixNest should support technicians with:

- Clear pricing rules.
- Job details before dispatch.
- Training materials.
- Quality feedback.
- Dispute mediation.
- Fair payment timelines.
- Growth opportunities for high performers.
