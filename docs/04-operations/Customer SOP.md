# FixNest Customer SOP

## Purpose

This SOP defines how FixNest should communicate with customers across booking, confirmation, service delivery, payment, complaints, refunds, and follow-up.

## Core Standards

- Confirm every booking with service category, location, visit window, pricing guidance, and support contact.
- Notify customers when a technician is assigned, delayed, rescheduled, or unable to arrive.
- Explain inspection fees, diagnostic charges, labor estimates, material charges, and cancellation rules clearly.
- Capture customer approval before non-standard or high-value work begins.
- Record payments, invoices, feedback, complaints, refunds, and follow-up actions.
- Escalate unresolved service issues through the operations team until closure.
