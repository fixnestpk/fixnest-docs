## Purpose

This SOP defines how FixNest should communicate with customers across booking, confirmation, service delivery, payment, complaints, refunds, and follow-up.

## Core Standards

- Confirm every booking with service category, location, visit window, pricing guidance, and support contact.
- Notify customers when a technician is assigned, delayed, rescheduled, or unable to arrive.
- Explain inspection fees, diagnostic charges, labor estimates, material charges, and cancellation rules clearly.
- Capture customer approval before non-standard or high-value work begins.
- Record payments, invoices, feedback, complaints, refunds, and follow-up actions.
- Escalate unresolved service issues through the operations team until closure.

## Customer Communication Stages

### 1. Inquiry

Collect the customer's name, phone number, address or service area, service category, issue description, preferred date, preferred time window, and any photos or videos that clarify the issue.

### 2. Booking Confirmation

Confirm the booking only after the customer understands:

- Service category.
- Visit window.
- Inspection or visit fee, if applicable.
- Pricing guidance or quotation process.
- Cancellation and rescheduling rules.
- Support contact.

### 3. Technician Assignment

Share technician name, service category, approximate arrival window, and any available identification or verification detail. Avoid sharing unnecessary private technician data.

### 4. Pre-Visit Reminder

Send a reminder before the visit when practical. Ask the customer to ensure site access, describe parking or entry instructions, and keep relevant appliances or fixtures accessible.

### 5. During Service

If the technician identifies additional work, the customer must approve the revised estimate before the work begins. Operations should be available to clarify pricing, scope, and safety concerns.

### 6. Completion

Before closure, confirm whether the work was completed, payment was collected, invoice details were recorded, photos or notes were saved if needed, and the customer understands any warranty or follow-up terms.

### 7. Follow-Up

Request feedback after completion. For low ratings or complaints, create a case and assign an owner.

## Complaint Handling

1. Acknowledge the complaint politely.
2. Record booking ID, customer details, technician details, issue type, evidence, and requested resolution.
3. Classify severity as low, medium, high, or urgent.
4. Contact the technician for a response when appropriate.
5. Offer next steps such as revisit, technical review, partial refund, escalation, or closure explanation.
6. Document final outcome.

## Refund and Rework Rules

Refunds and rework should be handled according to service policy, evidence, technician responsibility, customer approval history, and fairness. Operations should avoid immediate promises before reviewing the case.

## Communication Templates

### Booking Confirmation

Hello [Customer Name], your FixNest [Service Category] request is confirmed for [Date] between [Time Window] at [Area]. Estimated visit or inspection fee: [Amount]. Final work cost will be confirmed after inspection and your approval.

### Technician Assigned

Your FixNest technician [Technician Name] has been assigned for your [Service Category] booking. Expected arrival: [Time Window]. For support, contact [Support Contact].

### Delay Notice

We apologize for the delay. Your technician is currently expected at approximately [New Time]. Please reply if you need to reschedule.

### Completion Follow-Up

Thank you for using FixNest. Was your [Service Category] issue resolved today? Please rate the service from 1 to 5 and share any feedback.

## Quality Metrics

- Confirmation accuracy.
- Response time.
- On-time communication rate.
- Complaint acknowledgment time.
- Complaint resolution time.
- Customer satisfaction score.
- Repeat booking rate.
