# FixNest Service SOPs v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard service workflows FixNest should follow for customer bookings, technician assignment, service completion, invoicing, follow-up, complaints, and quality control.

## 2. Scope

These SOPs apply to all FixNest home service categories, beginning with electrician, plumber, and AC service jobs. The same structure should be adapted for future services such as CCTV, carpenter, painter, cleaning, pest control, solar, and renovation work.

## 3. Service Principles

Every FixNest service must follow these principles:

- Customer safety comes first.
- Technician identity and skill must be verified before assignment.
- Pricing must be explained before work begins where possible.
- Every job must have a traceable record.
- Work should be documented with notes, photos, invoices, and completion status.
- Complaints must be owned, tracked, and resolved through a defined process.
- Service quality must be reviewed continuously.

## 4. Standard Job Statuses

FixNest should track every job using the following statuses:

1. **Requested**: Customer has submitted a service request.
2. **Confirmed**: FixNest has validated the request and confirmed customer availability.
3. **Assigned**: A suitable technician has accepted the job.
4. **En Route**: Technician is travelling to the service location.
5. **In Progress**: Technician has started inspection or work.
6. **Quotation Shared**: Price has been communicated for non-standard work.
7. **Customer Approved**: Customer has approved scope and price.
8. **Completed**: Work has been completed and customer has been informed.
9. **Paid**: Payment has been collected or recorded.
10. **Closed**: Follow-up is complete and no issue remains open.
11. **Cancelled**: Job was cancelled before completion.
12. **Disputed**: Customer, technician, or operations team has raised an unresolved issue.

## 5. Booking Intake SOP

### 5.1 Required Customer Information

Operations staff or the booking system must collect:

- Customer name.
- Phone number.
- Service address.
- Service category.
- Issue description.
- Preferred date and time.
- Urgency level.
- Photos or videos if available.
- Access instructions for apartment, gate, society, or office building.

### 5.2 Request Validation

The operations team must verify:

- The address is within an active service area.
- The requested work belongs to an active service category.
- The issue is safe for a technician visit.
- The customer understands the visit charge, diagnostic charge, or minimum service fee if applicable.
- The customer is available at the requested time.

### 5.3 Booking Confirmation

After validation, FixNest should send confirmation through call, SMS, WhatsApp, email, or app notification including:

- Booking ID.
- Service category.
- Visit window.
- Initial pricing guidance.
- Customer support contact.
- Cancellation or rescheduling instructions.

## 6. Technician Assignment SOP

### 6.1 Assignment Criteria

Technicians should be assigned based on:

- Verified skill category.
- Location proximity.
- Availability.
- Rating and reliability score.
- Previous customer preference where applicable.
- Workload balance.
- Job complexity.

### 6.2 Technician Confirmation

Before assignment is finalized, the technician must confirm:

- Availability for the time slot.
- Ability to perform the requested service.
- Tools likely required.
- Estimated arrival time.
- Any safety concerns or material requirements.

### 6.3 Customer Technician Notification

The customer should receive:

- Technician name.
- Technician photo or ID where available.
- Expected arrival time.
- FixNest support number.
- Reminder to verify the technician before allowing entry.

## 7. Pre-Visit SOP

Technicians must:

- Wear or carry FixNest identification where available.
- Carry required tools and safety equipment.
- Review the job description before departure.
- Inform operations if delayed by more than 15 minutes.
- Avoid visiting without confirmed booking details.

Operations must:

- Monitor arrival delays.
- Inform customers of delays proactively.
- Reassign jobs when delays become unacceptable.

## 8. On-Site Service SOP

### 8.1 Arrival

Technicians must:

- Greet the customer professionally.
- Confirm the booking ID or customer name.
- Explain that inspection will be performed before final pricing for non-standard work.
- Respect household privacy, religious norms, and property rules.

### 8.2 Inspection

Technicians must:

- Inspect the issue carefully.
- Identify root cause where possible.
- Explain the problem in simple language.
- Share repair options if more than one solution exists.
- Inform the customer if materials or parts are required.

### 8.3 Quotation and Approval

Before starting chargeable work beyond the standard visit or diagnostic fee, the technician must:

- Share estimated labor cost.
- Share estimated material cost if applicable.
- Explain warranty or no-warranty conditions.
- Obtain customer approval.
- Inform operations when the quote is large, unusual, or outside standard pricing.

### 8.4 Work Execution

Technicians must:

- Use safe work practices.
- Avoid unnecessary damage to customer property.
- Keep the work area as clean as practical.
- Use appropriate materials and tools.
- Stop work and call operations if the job becomes unsafe or outside verified skill scope.

### 8.5 Completion

Before leaving, the technician must:

- Test the completed work.
- Show the result to the customer.
- Explain what was repaired or replaced.
- Share maintenance advice.
- Clean the immediate work area.
- Capture completion notes and photos where appropriate.

## 9. Service Category SOPs

### 9.1 Electrician Services

Common services include switch repair, wiring inspection, light installation, fan installation, breaker replacement, and power fault diagnosis.

Required precautions:

- Turn off power before touching wiring where required.
- Use proper testing tools.
- Do not perform work beyond safe capacity or legal requirements.
- Escalate major electrical faults, overheating, burning smell, or panel issues.

### 9.2 Plumbing Services

Common services include leakage repair, tap replacement, drain blockage, flush tank repair, pipe repair, and water motor checks.

Required precautions:

- Shut off water supply where needed.
- Identify hidden leakage risk before breaking walls or floors.
- Explain possible additional damage before invasive work.
- Test flow, leakage, and fittings after repair.

### 9.3 AC Services

Common services include AC general service, cooling diagnosis, gas leakage checks, drain cleaning, and minor repairs.

Required precautions:

- Use safe ladder and outdoor-unit handling practices.
- Confirm AC type and tonnage before visit where possible.
- Explain that gas charging requires leakage diagnosis.
- Do not guarantee cooling improvement if compressor, coil, gas leakage, or voltage issues remain unresolved.

## 10. Payment and Invoice SOP

FixNest should record:

- Labor charge.
- Visit or diagnostic charge.
- Material charge.
- Discounts or adjustments.
- Payment method.
- Payment status.
- Invoice number.

Customers should receive a digital invoice or written receipt. Cash collection must be reconciled with technician and operations records.

## 11. Follow-Up SOP

FixNest should follow up after service completion through call, WhatsApp, SMS, app, or email to collect:

- Customer satisfaction rating.
- Confirmation that work is functioning.
- Technician behavior feedback.
- Pricing transparency feedback.
- Any unresolved issue.

High-value, complaint-prone, or warranty-covered jobs should receive priority follow-up within 24 to 72 hours.

## 12. Complaint SOP

A complaint must be logged when the customer reports:

- Poor service quality.
- Technician delay or no-show.
- Unprofessional behavior.
- Overcharging or unclear pricing.
- Damage to property.
- Work failure after completion.
- Safety concern.

Each complaint record must include owner, status, severity, customer statement, technician statement, evidence, resolution action, and closing note.

## 13. Escalation SOP

Escalate immediately when:

- Customer safety is at risk.
- Technician safety is at risk.
- Property damage is reported.
- The customer threatens legal, police, or public complaint action.
- The job involves high-value equipment or major rework.
- The technician violates FixNest behavior standards.

Escalation owners should include operations lead, quality lead, and founder or general manager for severe cases.

## 14. Quality Control SOP

Quality checks should include:

- Random job audits.
- Call-back review for low ratings.
- Before-and-after photo review.
- Price variance review.
- Technician punctuality tracking.
- Repeat complaint analysis.
- Warranty claim analysis.

Technicians with repeated issues should receive coaching, restricted assignment, probation, or removal from the platform.

## 15. Cancellation and Rescheduling SOP

Customers may reschedule before technician dispatch without penalty unless a special arrangement has been made. If the technician is already en route, FixNest may apply a visit or cancellation fee depending on the customer communication and policy.

Technicians must not cancel directly with customers without informing operations.

## 16. Emergency Handling SOP

Emergency jobs include electrical burning smell, severe leakage, water overflow, AC electrical hazard, or urgent safety-related faults. Operations should:

- Confirm safety details.
- Advise the customer to shut off power, water, or equipment where appropriate.
- Assign a qualified technician only.
- Track technician arrival actively.
- Escalate if the job cannot be safely handled.

## 17. Records and Reporting

FixNest should maintain weekly reports covering:

- Total requests.
- Completed jobs.
- Cancelled jobs.
- Average response time.
- Average completion time.
- Revenue by category.
- Complaints by category.
- Customer ratings.
- Technician performance.
