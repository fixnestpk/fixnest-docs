# FixNest Operations Manual v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This manual explains how FixNest should operate its home services business from customer request to service completion, quality control, reporting, and continuous improvement.

## 2. Operating Model

FixNest acts as the trusted operating layer between homeowners and verified technicians. The operations team manages demand, supply, scheduling, pricing guidance, customer communication, quality assurance, and complaint resolution.

## 3. Core Teams

### 3.1 Customer Support

Responsible for booking intake, customer communication, service updates, follow-up, and basic complaint logging.

### 3.2 Dispatch and Scheduling

Responsible for technician assignment, arrival tracking, rescheduling, route coordination, and workload balancing.

### 3.3 Technician Network

Responsible for delivering services according to FixNest standards.

### 3.4 Quality Assurance

Responsible for audits, complaint review, technician performance, warranty cases, and process improvement.

### 3.5 Finance and Reconciliation

Responsible for payment tracking, technician payouts, cash reconciliation, invoice records, and margin reporting.

### 3.6 Growth and Marketing

Responsible for demand generation, campaigns, referral programs, content, partnerships, and customer retention.

## 4. Daily Operating Workflow

1. Review open bookings.
2. Confirm new requests.
3. Assign technicians.
4. Track dispatch and arrival.
5. Monitor in-progress jobs.
6. Approve or review unusual quotations.
7. Confirm completion and payment.
8. Follow up with customers.
9. Resolve open complaints.
10. Update daily dashboard.

## 5. Booking Management

Every booking must include customer details, service category, address, issue description, preferred time, assigned technician, status, pricing notes, payment status, and follow-up result.

Bookings should not remain unassigned beyond the defined response target unless capacity is unavailable. Unassigned urgent jobs should be escalated.

## 6. Technician Supply Management

FixNest should maintain enough verified technicians per service area and category to cover expected demand. Technician records should include identity, skills, service areas, availability, rating, completed jobs, complaints, documents, and payout terms.

## 7. Scheduling Rules

Scheduling should consider:

- Customer preferred time.
- Technician proximity.
- Job duration.
- Skill match.
- Urgency.
- Traffic and travel time.
- Previous technician-customer relationship.
- Technician reliability score.

## 8. Customer Communication Standards

Customers should receive clear updates at these points:

- Booking received.
- Booking confirmed.
- Technician assigned.
- Technician delayed or rescheduled.
- Quote shared.
- Job completed.
- Invoice or payment confirmation.
- Follow-up request.

Communication should be polite, simple, and transparent.

## 9. Technician Communication Standards

Technicians must receive complete job details before dispatch and must update operations at major job milestones. Operations should avoid vague instructions and should not assign technicians outside their verified skills.

## 10. Quality Management

FixNest should run quality checks through:

- Customer ratings.
- Follow-up calls.
- Photo reviews.
- Complaint analysis.
- Rework tracking.
- Price variance reviews.
- Technician scorecards.

Low-quality jobs should trigger coaching, process updates, or technician restrictions.

## 11. Complaint Management

Every complaint must have:

- Complaint ID.
- Booking ID.
- Customer details.
- Technician details.
- Complaint category.
- Severity level.
- Owner.
- Target resolution time.
- Resolution note.
- Customer confirmation before closure where possible.

## 12. Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Standard Issue

Minor delay, minor dissatisfaction, or simple clarification. Handled by support.

### Level 2: Service Failure

Poor work, repeat issue, no-show, or pricing dispute. Handled by operations lead or quality lead.

### Level 3: Serious Incident

Safety issue, property damage, harassment, fraud, or legal threat. Handled by senior management immediately.

## 13. Payment Operations

FixNest should track all payments by booking. Cash payments collected by technicians require reconciliation. Digital payments should be matched to invoices. Technician payouts should be based on completed and verified jobs, minus applicable commission, penalties, refunds, or adjustments.

## 14. Data and Reporting

Daily dashboard should include:

- New requests.
- Confirmed bookings.
- Completed jobs.
- Cancelled jobs.
- Revenue.
- Gross margin.
- Open complaints.
- Average response time.
- Technician availability.

Weekly management review should include category performance, customer satisfaction, pricing issues, technician scorecards, and growth opportunities.

## 15. Risk Controls

Key controls include:

- Technician verification before assignment.
- Customer confirmation before dispatch.
- Price approval before major work.
- Complaint escalation rules.
- Cash reconciliation.
- Data privacy discipline.
- Safety-first job refusal for unsafe requests.

## 16. Launch Readiness Checklist

Before launch, FixNest should have:

- Active service categories defined.
- Verified technician pool.
- Pricing master sheet.
- Booking form or workflow.
- Customer support number or channel.
- Technician SOPs.
- Service SOPs.
- Complaint tracker.
- Invoice or receipt process.
- Daily dashboard.

## 17. Continuous Improvement

Operations should improve through weekly review of failures, feedback, complaints, and revenue data. Every repeated problem should become a process update, training item, pricing adjustment, or technology requirement.
