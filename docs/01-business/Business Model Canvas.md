# FixNest Business Model Canvas

Status: Draft

---

## Customer Segments

- Homeowners, tenants, families, working professionals, small offices, and property managers.
- Verified electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, and future service professionals.
- Future suppliers of home-related products and materials.

## Value Propositions

- Trusted, verified home service professionals.
- Transparent booking, pricing guidance, service records, and support.
- A single home ecosystem for repairs, maintenance, products, education, and future AI assistance.

## Channels

- Website, WhatsApp, phone support, social media, local SEO, paid search, referrals, and partnerships.

## Customer Relationships

- Assisted booking and support during launch.
- Follow-up, complaint handling, ratings, reminders, and repeat booking.
- Future self-service accounts and personalized maintenance journeys.

## Revenue Streams

- Service margin or commission, visit fees, diagnostic fees, emergency premiums, material margin, annual maintenance contracts, memberships, and future marketplace revenue.

## Key Resources

- Verified technician network, operations team, customer support, brand trust, service data, SOPs, platform tools, and supplier relationships.

## Key Activities

- Technician onboarding, booking management, dispatch, quality assurance, pricing governance, customer support, marketing, and platform development.

## Key Partners

- Technicians, suppliers, housing societies, property managers, hardware stores, payment providers, and future insurance or warranty partners.

## Cost Structure

- Technician payouts, support operations, marketing, technology, verification, training, refunds, warranty rework, administration, legal, and finance.
