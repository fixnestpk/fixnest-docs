# FixNest Mission

Version: 0.1.0  
Status: Draft  
Owner: Founders  
Last Updated: 2026-08-02

---

To simplify home maintenance for homeowners by connecting them with verified professionals, transparent pricing, reliable customer support, service history, and technology-driven convenience.

## Mission Commitments

- Verify and monitor service professionals before they enter the FixNest network.
- Standardize booking, dispatch, service delivery, invoicing, complaints, and follow-up.
- Give customers clearer expectations before, during, and after each service visit.
- Help technicians access structured work, better reputation, and fair earning opportunities.
- Build operational processes that are repeatable, measurable, and teachable as the company scales.
- Use technology to reduce confusion, delays, missed communication, and preventable service failures.

## What the Mission Means in Practice

FixNest should not act as a simple lead generator. The company should take responsibility for shaping the customer experience from the moment a service need is reported until the issue is closed. This means every booking needs clear ownership, every technician needs standards, every customer needs a support path, and every completed job should improve the system's knowledge.

The mission also requires balance. Customers need trust, speed, and fair pricing. Technicians need respect, predictable work, and clear expectations. FixNest must design policies that serve both sides while protecting the long-term reputation of the platform.

## Operating Principles

### Trust Before Growth

FixNest should not expand faster than it can maintain quality. Adding more technicians, cities, or service categories is valuable only when verification, training, dispatch, support, and complaint handling can keep up.

### Clarity Before Commitment

Customers should understand the service category, visit window, expected inspection fee, estimated price range, payment method, cancellation rules, and support channel before confirming a booking.

### Accountability After Completion

A job is not truly complete until the customer confirms closure, payment is recorded, technician notes are saved, and any complaint or warranty concern has a clear follow-up path.

## Near-Term Mission Priorities

1. Launch with a focused set of essential categories: electrician, plumber, and AC service.
2. Build a dependable technician onboarding and verification workflow.
3. Maintain a simple but complete booking and job tracking system.
4. Publish basic pricing guidance for common jobs.
5. Create consistent customer communication templates for every service stage.
6. Collect ratings, complaints, and operational lessons after each job.

## Long-Term Mission Outcomes

- Customers think of FixNest first when something in the home needs repair, maintenance, installation, or inspection.
- Technicians see FixNest as a professional network that rewards quality and reliability.
- Operations teams can manage service quality using data, SOPs, and clear escalation rules.
- FixNest becomes a foundation for future services, home products, maintenance plans, and AI-assisted home support.
