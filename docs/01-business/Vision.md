# FixNest Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted Home Ecosystem by providing every essential home service, home improvement solution, and home-related product through one reliable platform.

## Vision Principles

- Make home ownership safer, easier, faster, and more reliable.
- Build long-term trust through verified professionals and accountable service delivery.
- Create one recognizable platform for home repairs, maintenance, products, education, and support.
- Use technology and operational discipline to improve every customer and technician interaction.
