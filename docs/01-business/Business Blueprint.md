## 1. Executive Summary

FixNest is a trusted Home Ecosystem platform for Pakistan that connects homeowners with verified professionals, reliable home services, and quality home-related products through one unified platform.

FixNest will begin with essential services such as electrical work, plumbing, and AC service, then expand into broader home maintenance, home improvement, product marketplace, annual maintenance contracts, emergency support, and AI-powered home management.

The long-term ambition is to make FixNest the first platform homeowners think of whenever they need anything related to their home.

## 2. Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted Home Ecosystem by providing every essential home service, home improvement solution, and home-related product through one reliable platform.

## 3. Mission

To simplify home maintenance for homeowners by connecting them with verified professionals, transparent pricing, reliable customer support, service history, and technology-driven convenience.

## 4. Problem Statement

Homeowners in Pakistan often face the following problems when managing home repairs and maintenance:

- Difficulty finding trusted technicians quickly.
- No reliable verification of workers or service providers.
- Different prices from different people with limited transparency.
- Poor service quality and inconsistent customer experience.
- No proper warranty, guarantee, or accountability after work is completed.
- No centralized work history or customer profile.
- Limited complaint management or escalation process.
- Fragmented services across many individual providers.
- Lack of preventive maintenance guidance.
- Higher risk during emergency or disaster situations.

## 5. Our Solution

FixNest solves these problems by acting as a trusted layer between homeowners and service providers.

The platform will provide:

- Verified professional profiles.
- Customer profiles and saved service history.
- Transparent service processes and pricing guidance.
- Easy booking through one platform.
- Standard operating procedures for service delivery.
- Customer support, complaint handling, and escalation workflows.
- Ratings, reviews, and quality control.
- Service guarantees and warranties where applicable.
- Maintenance reminders and educational content.
- AI-compatible workflows for instant responses, estimates, and recommendations.

## 6. Core Value Proposition

FixNest is a trusted Home Ecosystem that makes home maintenance safer, easier, faster, and more reliable by combining verified professionals, transparent processes, intelligent technology, and exceptional customer support into one unified platform.

Customers should choose FixNest instead of calling a local technician directly because FixNest provides brand-level trust, accountability, transparency, support, and a complete system around the service experience.

## 9. Business Model

### Initial Model

1. Homeowner books a service.
2. FixNest receives the request.
3. FixNest assigns a suitable technician.
4. Technician completes the job.
5. Customer pays for the service.
6. FixNest earns a commission or service margin.

### Future Revenue Streams
