# FixNest Legal and Compliance Plan

Version: 0.1.0  
Status: Draft  
Owner: Legal  
Last Updated: 2026-08-02

---

## Purpose

This document outlines the legal, compliance, policy, and risk areas FixNest should address before and during launch. It is a planning document and is not legal advice. Final policies and contracts should be reviewed by qualified counsel.

## Priority Legal Documents

- Customer terms of service.
- Privacy policy.
- Technician onboarding agreement.
- Technician code of conduct.
- Service warranty or guarantee policy.
- Refund and cancellation policy.
- Complaint and dispute resolution policy.
- Supplier agreement for future marketplace operations.
- Internal data handling policy.

## Customer Policy Topics

- Scope of FixNest's role in arranging and managing services.
- Booking, cancellation, rescheduling, and no-show rules.
- Inspection fees, labor charges, material charges, and payment terms.
- Customer approval requirements for additional work.
- Limits of warranty or service guarantee.
- Complaint submission process and evidence requirements.
- Refund eligibility and timelines.
- Customer responsibilities for safe access, accurate information, and payment.

## Technician Policy Topics

- Verification and onboarding requirements.
- Skill categories and permitted work.
- Professional conduct inside customer premises.
- Attendance, punctuality, and communication rules.
- Pricing, estimate, and payment collection rules.
- Documentation requirements for job notes, photos, invoices, and materials.
- Complaint cooperation and rework obligations.
- Grounds for suspension or removal from the network.

## Data and Privacy Considerations

FixNest may collect customer names, phone numbers, addresses, service history, complaint records, payment records, technician profiles, identity verification details, ratings, photos, and support communications. Data collection should be limited to legitimate operational needs.

Key controls should include:

- Clear privacy notice.
- Role-based access to customer and technician data.
- Secure storage of identity documents if collected.
- Data retention rules.
- Customer request handling for correction or deletion where applicable.
- Vendor review for payment, messaging, support, and analytics tools.

## Operational Risk Areas

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Property damage | Technician standards, evidence capture, complaint process, insurance review |
| Unsafe technician behavior | Verification, code of conduct, customer escalation, suspension rules |
| Pricing dispute | Written estimates, approval before additional work, invoice records |
| Failed repair | Warranty rules, revisit workflow, quality review |
| Data misuse | Access controls, audit logs, privacy policy, staff training |
| Payment dispute | Clear payment records, refund policy, support review |

## Insurance and Liability Review

Before scaling, FixNest should explore insurance options for operational liability, worker incidents, property damage claims, cyber/data risk, and business interruption. Coverage needs may vary by legal structure, service categories, and whether technicians are employees, contractors, or marketplace partners.

## Compliance Backlog

1. Confirm company registration and operating structure.
2. Draft customer-facing terms and privacy policy.
3. Draft technician onboarding agreement and code of conduct.
4. Define refund, cancellation, warranty, and dispute policies.
5. Review payment collection and tax documentation requirements.
6. Define data retention and deletion policy.
7. Review insurance needs before high-volume operations.
