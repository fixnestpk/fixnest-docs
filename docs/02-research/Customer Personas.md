## Purpose

This document defines the initial customer segments FixNest should design for. Personas are working assumptions until validated through interviews, booking data, support logs, and market testing.

## Persona 1: Busy Homeowner or Tenant

### Profile

- Lives in an apartment, house, or rented unit.
- Has limited time to search for technicians.
- Often needs electrical, plumbing, AC, or appliance-related help.
- Uses WhatsApp, calls, and search when looking for services.

### Goals

- Get the problem fixed quickly.
- Avoid repeated calls and unreliable arrivals.
- Know who is coming and when.
- Pay a fair price without argument.

### Pain Points

- Technicians may not answer, arrive late, or cancel.
- Quality is unpredictable.
- Pricing changes after the technician arrives.
- There is no service record for future reference.

### FixNest Opportunity

Provide quick assisted booking, clear confirmation, technician assignment details, and follow-up after service completion.

## Persona 2: Safety-Conscious Family Household

### Profile

- Family decision maker responsible for home safety.
- Concerned about allowing unknown workers inside the home.
- Values professionalism, identity verification, and polite conduct.

### Goals

- Use trusted workers for home access.
- Reduce safety risk for family members.
- Receive clear communication before visit.
- Have a complaint path if something goes wrong.

### Pain Points

- Informal referrals do not guarantee accountability.
- Service workers may not follow professional behavior standards.
- Family members may feel uncomfortable coordinating alone.

### FixNest Opportunity

Emphasize verified profiles, visit confirmations, code of conduct, support contact, and customer feedback.

## Persona 3: Price-Sensitive Customer

### Profile

- Compares prices before booking.
- May delay maintenance if costs feel uncertain.
- Wants to avoid hidden charges and unnecessary work.

### Goals

- Understand likely cost before approving service.
- Separate inspection, labor, and material charges.
- Receive invoice or payment record.

### Pain Points

- No standard market pricing.
- Technicians may quote differently for similar jobs.
- Material quality and markup are unclear.

### FixNest Opportunity

Publish price ranges, define quote approval rules, and document charges after completion.

## Persona 4: Preventive Maintenance Planner

### Profile

- Owns a home, manages recurring maintenance, or wants fewer emergency repairs.
- Interested in reminders, seasonal checks, and annual plans.

### Goals

- Keep home systems working reliably.
- Avoid emergency breakdowns.
- Track what was repaired and when.

### Pain Points

- Maintenance is forgotten until failure occurs.
- No single record of previous work.
- Hard to know when AC, electrical, plumbing, or safety checks are due.

### FixNest Opportunity

Create service history, reminders, checklists, and annual maintenance packages.

## Persona 5: Property Manager or Small Office

### Profile

- Coordinates repairs for rental units, small offices, shops, or shared properties.
- Needs documentation, repeat service, and clear accountability.

### Goals

- Book reliable technicians across multiple locations.
- Track service spend and recurring issues.
- Coordinate between tenants, owners, and workers.

### Pain Points

- Multiple stakeholders make communication difficult.
- Poor documentation causes disputes.
- Repeat issues are not tracked systematically.

### FixNest Opportunity

Offer multi-location profiles, service records, invoices, priority support, and recurring maintenance options.

## Research Questions to Validate

- Which service categories are most urgent in launch cities?
- What level of inspection fee is acceptable before customers hesitate?
- Which trust signals matter most: ID verification, reviews, photos, uniform, support call, or guarantee?
- How much do customers prefer WhatsApp-assisted booking versus app or website self-service?
- What complaint outcomes do customers consider fair?
