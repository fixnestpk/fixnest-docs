## Primary Personas

1. Busy homeowner or tenant who needs fast, reliable repairs.
2. Safety-conscious family household that prioritizes verified workers.
3. Price-sensitive homeowner who wants transparent estimates and invoices.
4. Preventive maintenance planner who values reminders and service history.
5. Property manager or small office that needs recurring, documented support.

Detailed persona research should be expanded with interviews, service scenarios, decision drivers, objections, and channel preferences.
