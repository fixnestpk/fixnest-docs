## Purpose

This document captures working research about home repair, maintenance, and home services demand in Pakistan. It should be updated as FixNest collects customer interviews, service bookings, competitor observations, pricing samples, and city-level operating data.

## Research Scope

FixNest's initial market is urban and semi-urban households that need reliable home repair and maintenance services. Launch research should focus on categories with frequent demand, urgent pain, and manageable operational complexity.

## Priority Service Categories

1. Electrical repair and installation.
2. Plumbing repair and installation.
3. AC service, maintenance, and repair.
4. Appliance repair.
5. CCTV and smart security installation.
6. Cleaning, pest control, and painting.
7. Renovation, carpentry, and fixture installation.

## Customer Pain Points

- Difficulty finding a reliable technician at the right time.
- Unclear prices and inconsistent quotes.
- Fear of unsafe or unverified workers entering the home.
- Poor punctuality and weak communication.
- No structured complaint process.
- No digital invoice, warranty note, or service history.
- Lack of guidance about preventive maintenance.

## Demand Patterns to Track

| Pattern | Expected Impact | Data to Collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer AC demand | Higher AC servicing and emergency repair volume | Category requests by month |
| Rainy season plumbing/electrical issues | More leakage, seepage, short-circuit, and drainage problems | Issue type and urgency |
| Weekends and evenings | Higher preference for non-working-hour visits | Requested time slots |
| Move-in/move-out periods | Installation, inspection, and repair bundles | Customer reason for booking |
| Festivals and holidays | Cleaning, fixture, lighting, and urgent repair demand | Campaign performance |

## Target Launch Geographies

City and neighborhood selection should be based on:

- Residential density.
- Technician availability.
- Ability to reach customers within reliable time windows.
- Digital booking behavior.
- Safety and accessibility for technicians.
- Presence of apartments, housing societies, and middle-income households.

## Research Methods

- Interview homeowners, tenants, property managers, and technicians.
- Collect price samples for common services across neighborhoods.
- Observe competitor booking flows, guarantees, categories, and customer reviews.
- Run small landing page or WhatsApp booking experiments.
- Track abandoned bookings and reasons for non-conversion.
- Review complaint themes from completed jobs.

## Metrics to Establish Baseline

- Booking conversion rate.
- Average response time.
- Technician acceptance rate.
- On-time arrival rate.
- Completion rate.
- Complaint rate.
- Repeat booking rate.
- Average order value.
- Gross margin per category.
- Customer satisfaction score.

## Open Questions

- Which customer segment has the strongest willingness to pay for verified service?
- Should FixNest lead with emergency repairs, scheduled maintenance, or annual plans?
- Which service categories produce the best balance of demand, margin, and quality control?
- What is the minimum customer support process needed to create trust at launch?
- Which neighborhoods can support dense technician coverage first?
