## Purpose

This document will capture research about home repair, maintenance, and home services demand in Pakistan.

## Research Areas

- Target cities and neighborhoods.
- Customer pain points and service frequency.
- Common categories: electrical, plumbing, AC service, appliance repair, cleaning, pest control, and renovation.
- Seasonal demand patterns.
- Pricing expectations and willingness to pay for verified service.
- Safety, trust, and household access concerns.
- Digital booking behavior and preferred communication channels.
