## Purpose

This document will compare FixNest with direct, indirect, and substitute solutions in the home services market.

## Competitor Categories

- Local individual technicians.
- Informal referral networks.
- Social media groups and WhatsApp contacts.
- Existing home service marketplaces.
- Appliance brand service centers.
- Facility management providers.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Trust and verification model.
- Service categories and coverage areas.
- Pricing transparency.
- Booking experience.
- Technician quality control.
- Complaint handling and refunds.
- Service guarantees or warranties.
- Customer reviews and brand perception.
