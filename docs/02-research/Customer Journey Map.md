## 1. Purpose

This Customer Journey Map defines how homeowners experience FixNest from the moment they discover the brand through booking, technician assignment, service completion, payment, support, repeat booking, and future preventive maintenance.

The document translates FixNest's positioning as Pakistan's trusted Home Ecosystem into a practical service journey. It is intended to guide product design, operations, customer support, technician workflows, pricing communication, content planning, and future AI-enabled experiences.

The journey should strengthen FixNest's five brand promises at every stage:

1. **Trust**: Verified, accountable professionals backed by FixNest.
2. **Convenience**: Home services available through one unified platform.
3. **Transparency**: Clear pricing guidance, job updates, invoices, and service history.
4. **Quality**: Standard service delivery, quality checks, reviews, and complaint handling.
5. **Innovation**: Technology, AI, reminders, and intelligent guidance that simplify home ownership.

## 2. Primary customer personas

### 2.1 Busy homeowner or tenant

**Profile:** A working adult or family decision-maker who needs quick, reliable help for home repairs but has limited time to search for technicians.

**Needs:** Fast booking, reliable arrival windows, clear communication, trustworthy workers, and minimal follow-up effort.

**Concerns:** Wasted time, late arrivals, unclear pricing, poor workmanship, and safety inside the home.

### 2.2 Safety-conscious family household

**Profile:** A household that prioritizes verified workers, accountability, and service quality because technicians enter private family spaces.

**Needs:** Identity-verified professionals, visible ratings, support escalation, service guarantees where applicable, and clear technician assignment details.

**Concerns:** Unknown workers, weak background checks, no support after service, and limited accountability if something goes wrong.

### 2.3 Price-sensitive homeowner

**Profile:** A customer who wants repairs completed correctly but worries about inconsistent market pricing and unexpected charges.

**Needs:** Pricing guidance, inspection fees where applicable, pre-work estimates, digital quotations for non-standard jobs, and invoices.

**Concerns:** Overcharging, hidden costs, unnecessary parts replacement, and no proof of payment.

### 2.4 Preventive maintenance planner

**Profile:** A homeowner who wants to avoid breakdowns through seasonal service, reminders, service history, and future annual maintenance plans.

**Needs:** Maintenance calendar, reminders, historical records, repeat booking, trusted technician preferences, and recommendations.

**Concerns:** Forgetting essential maintenance, emergency costs, losing previous service records, and not knowing when to service key home systems.

## 3. End-to-end homeowner journey

At a high level, the homeowner journey moves through the following lifecycle:

1. **Discovery:** The homeowner becomes aware of FixNest through search, social media, referral, educational content, or local marketing.
2. **Evaluation:** The homeowner checks whether FixNest is trustworthy, available in their area, and suitable for the required service.
3. **Booking:** The homeowner submits service type, issue details, location, preferred time, and contact information.
4. **Confirmation:** FixNest confirms the request, communicates pricing guidance or inspection rules, and sets expectations.
5. **Technician assignment:** FixNest assigns a verified technician based on skill, location, availability, rating, and workload.
6. **Service visit:** The technician arrives, diagnoses the issue, explains work required, confirms cost where possible, performs the job, and records completion details.
7. **Payment and invoice:** The customer pays through the supported payment method and receives a payment record or digital invoice.
8. **Review and support:** The customer rates the service, raises any complaint if needed, and receives support or escalation.
9. **Service history:** FixNest stores the job record, invoice, complaint status, technician details, and review.
10. **Repeat booking and reminders:** FixNest encourages repeat usage through saved addresses, previous service records, maintenance reminders, and future annual maintenance plans.

## 4. Discovery stage

### Customer goal

Find a reliable solution for a home repair, maintenance issue, or planned service without relying on random contacts or unverified local providers.

### Customer actions

- Searches online for an electrician, plumber, or AC technician.
- Asks family, neighbors, or social media groups for recommendations.
- Sees FixNest content, ads, website pages, service guides, or referral messages.
- Compares FixNest against calling a local technician directly.

### Customer questions

- Is FixNest available in my area?
- Are the technicians verified?
- What services are offered?
- How much will it cost?
- What happens if the technician does not arrive or the work fails later?

### FixNest touchpoints

- Website or landing page.
- Search results and service category pages.
- Social media posts.
- Educational articles, maintenance guides, safety checklists, and money-saving tips.
- WhatsApp, phone, or form-based inquiry channels in early phases.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Clearly communicate that FixNest starts with electrician, plumber, and AC service.
- Highlight verified professionals, transparent processes, support, reviews, and service history.
- Provide simple service explanations and pricing guidance where possible.
- Make booking entry points visible and easy to use.

## 5. Service booking stage

### Customer goal

Request the right service quickly with enough information for FixNest to understand the issue and arrange help.

### Customer actions

- Selects or states the required service category.
- Provides address, contact details, preferred time, and issue description.
- Shares photos, videos, or notes if supported.
- Confirms whether the issue is urgent, routine, inspection-based, or quotation-based.

### Required booking information

- Customer name.
- Phone number.
- Service address.
- Service type.
- Issue description.
- Preferred date and time.
- Urgency level.
- Access notes or special instructions.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Keep the booking flow short and understandable.
- Capture enough information to assign the right technician.
- Explain that final pricing may depend on inspection for non-standard jobs.
- Create a traceable job record from the first request.

## 6. Confirmation and technician assignment stage

### Customer goal

Receive confidence that the request has been accepted, expectations are clear, and a suitable technician is assigned.

### Customer actions

- Reviews booking confirmation.
- Confirms timing, location, and service scope.
- Receives technician details when assigned.
- Waits for arrival updates.

### FixNest operations actions

- Validate the request details.
- Confirm service area and availability.
- Assign a technician based on verified skill category, location, availability, rating, workload, and service fit.
- Share confirmation, estimated arrival time, technician name, and contact or tracking details where appropriate.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Communicate job status such as requested, confirmed, assigned, in progress, completed, cancelled, or disputed.
- Avoid assigning technicians outside verified skill categories.
- Provide clear instructions if rescheduling or cancellation is required.
- Maintain internal ownership for every active booking.

## 7. Service visit stage

### Customer goal

Have the home issue diagnosed and resolved safely, professionally, and with clear communication.

### Customer actions

- Allows technician access to the service area.
- Explains the issue in more detail.
- Reviews diagnosis, price estimate, parts needs, or work scope.
- Approves the work before major activity begins.
- Confirms completion after the technician finishes.

### Technician actions

- Arrives within the promised window or informs FixNest of delay.
- Confirms customer and job details.
- Diagnoses the issue.
- Explains required work in simple language.
- Communicates pricing or quotation needs before proceeding where possible.
- Completes the work according to FixNest service standards.
- Records completion notes, parts used, before-and-after photos where appropriate, and customer confirmation.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Standardize technician behavior, communication, safety, and work documentation.
- Support before-and-after photos for transparency and quality control where applicable.
- Escalate unclear pricing, expanded scope, safety concerns, or customer dissatisfaction during the visit.
- Preserve service history for future support and repeat booking.

## 8. Payment and invoice stage

### Customer goal

Pay the correct amount and receive a clear record of charges and completed work.

### Customer actions

- Reviews final charges.
- Pays using the available payment method.
- Receives receipt, invoice, or payment confirmation.
- Keeps the record for future reference, warranty, complaint, or maintenance history.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Provide transparent pricing guidance for standard services.
- Use custom quotations for non-standard work.
- Provide digital invoices and payment records as the platform matures.
- Record payment status against the job.
- Ensure charges match the communicated service scope and any approved changes.

## 9. Review, complaint, and support stage

### Customer goal

Confirm satisfaction, share feedback, and receive help if the service was late, incomplete, overpriced, unsafe, or poor quality.

### Customer actions

- Rates the technician and service experience.
- Leaves written feedback.
- Contacts support if there is a complaint.
- Shares evidence such as photos, invoice, or service notes if needed.

### FixNest support actions

- Capture customer rating and review.
- Open a complaint record with owner, status, and resolution notes when needed.
- Escalate issues according to complaint severity.
- Review technician performance and repeat complaint patterns.
- Apply service guarantee or warranty rules where applicable.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Make support visible and easy to reach.
- Treat complaint handling as a trust-building moment, not just a cost center.
- Close the loop with the customer after resolution.
- Use reviews and complaints to improve technician scoring, training, and quality control.

## 10. Repeat booking and maintenance reminder stage

### Customer goal

Use FixNest again without repeating all information and receive timely guidance that prevents future home issues.

### Customer actions

- Books the same or related service again.
- Selects saved address and previous service category.
- Requests a preferred or favorite technician where supported.
- Receives seasonal reminders such as AC servicing before summer or plumbing inspection after recurring leakage.
- Considers annual maintenance contracts in future phases.

### FixNest experience requirements

- Store customer profile, addresses, service history, invoices, complaints, reviews, and preferences.
- Use service history to make repeat booking faster.
- Recommend preventive maintenance based on prior jobs and seasonal needs.
- Support maintenance reminders and annual maintenance plans in future phases.
- Build long-term relationship value beyond one-time repair transactions.

## 11. Customer pain points at each stage

| Stage | Common pain points |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Customers do not know which technician to trust, search is fragmented, and service quality is uncertain. |
| Evaluation | Customers cannot easily compare verification, pricing, reviews, warranties, and support accountability. |
| Booking | Long forms, unclear service categories, missing response confirmation, and difficulty explaining the issue. |
| Confirmation | Unclear arrival time, no technician details, weak status updates, and fear that no one will show up. |
| Technician assignment | Skill mismatch, technician unavailability, unknown worker identity, and inconsistent communication. |
| Service visit | Late arrival, unclear diagnosis, unexpected charges, safety concerns, poor workmanship, and no work documentation. |
| Payment and invoice | No itemized record, unclear parts charges, cash-only friction, and no proof for future warranty or complaint. |
| Review and complaint | No easy escalation path, slow support, no owner for the issue, and no resolution record. |
| Repeat booking | Customer must explain the same issue again, previous records are lost, and no reminder for preventive maintenance. |

## 12. FixNest opportunities to improve trust, convenience, transparency, quality, and innovation

### Trust

- Verify technician identity, skills, service areas, documents, and certifications where applicable.
- Display ratings, completed job history, and customer reviews.
- Ensure every complaint has an owner, status, and resolution note.
- Offer service guarantees or warranty options for eligible services.

### Convenience

- Provide one booking path for electrician, plumber, and AC service in Phase 1.
- Save customer profiles, addresses, service history, and preferences.
- Enable repeat booking and favorite technicians in later phases.
- Add WhatsApp, web, app, and voice booking as the platform matures.

### Transparency

- Publish pricing guidance for standard services.
- Use inspection-based estimates or custom quotations for non-standard jobs.
- Share booking status, technician assignment, estimated arrival time, and completion records.
- Provide digital invoices, before-and-after photos, and service reports.

### Quality

- Use standard operating procedures for each service category.
- Track technician reliability, ratings, complaint rate, and job completion quality.
- Audit completed jobs and repeat complaints.
- Expand service categories only when operations can support quality.

### Innovation

- Design workflows and data models that support future AI compatibility.
- Add AI triage, AI cost estimation, AI technician matching, predictive maintenance, and smart reminders.
- Use educational content, maintenance guides, and energy-saving advice to reduce avoidable service problems.
- Build toward a unified home management system rather than a simple technician directory.

## 13. Phase 1 journey for electrician, plumber, and AC service

Phase 1 should keep the journey focused, controlled, and operationally realistic. Booking may be manual or semi-manual, but the customer experience should still feel structured and accountable.

### 13.1 Electrician journey

1. Customer discovers FixNest while searching for help with wiring, switch, socket, light, breaker, fan, or appliance connection issues.
2. Customer books electrician service and provides issue details, photos if available, address, preferred time, and urgency.
3. FixNest confirms the request and assigns a verified electrician within the service area.
4. Electrician arrives, inspects the issue, explains safety risks, confirms scope, and communicates estimated cost before work where possible.
5. Electrician completes the repair or installation, tests the work, and records completion notes.
6. Customer pays, receives payment record or invoice, and submits a review.
7. FixNest stores the service history and may later recommend safety checks or preventive maintenance.

### 13.2 Plumber journey

1. Customer discovers FixNest for leakage, blockage, water pressure, tap, pipe, washroom, kitchen, or tank-related issues.
2. Customer books plumber service with location, issue description, urgency, photos or video where useful, and preferred time.
3. FixNest confirms whether the job appears standard, inspection-based, or quotation-based.
4. FixNest assigns a verified plumber based on skill, location, availability, rating, and workload.
5. Plumber diagnoses the issue, explains whether parts are needed, confirms cost expectations, and performs approved work.
6. Customer reviews the completed work, pays, receives a record, and rates the service.
7. FixNest tracks recurring issues and may later suggest preventive checks, replacement guidance, or annual maintenance.

### 13.3 AC service journey

1. Customer discovers FixNest before summer, during cooling problems, water leakage, noise, servicing needs, or AC breakdowns.
2. Customer books AC service with AC type, issue, brand or tonnage if known, address, preferred time, and urgency.
3. FixNest confirms the request, sets expectations for standard service versus repair diagnosis, and assigns a verified AC technician.
4. Technician inspects the unit, explains required servicing or repair, communicates cost or quotation needs, and completes approved work.
5. Technician tests cooling, drainage, and basic performance after service.
6. Customer pays, receives invoice or payment record, and submits feedback.
7. FixNest stores service history and later sends seasonal reminders for AC cleaning, servicing, or maintenance.

## 14. Future-state journey with app, AI assistant, cost estimator, and maintenance reminders

In the future-state journey, FixNest becomes a full home management companion rather than only a booking channel.

### Future-state journey flow

1. **App onboarding:** Customer creates a profile, saves addresses, adds home details, and optionally records major appliances or systems.
2. **AI assistant triage:** Customer describes the issue through chat, WhatsApp, voice, photo, or video. The AI assistant asks follow-up questions, suggests safety steps, and routes the request to the right service category.
3. **Cost estimator:** Customer receives a pricing range or estimate for common jobs, with clear notes about inspection-based or parts-dependent pricing.
4. **Smart scheduling:** Customer sees available slots, estimated arrival windows, technician options where supported, and urgency-based booking.
5. **AI-assisted technician matching:** FixNest recommends technicians based on verified skills, location, rating, availability, workload, prior customer preference, and job complexity.
6. **Live job updates:** Customer receives real-time confirmation, assignment, arrival, in-progress, completion, and payment updates.
7. **Digital service report:** Customer receives before-and-after photos, work notes, parts details, invoice, warranty information where applicable, and future recommendations.
8. **Maintenance reminders:** FixNest sends reminders for AC servicing, electrical safety checks, plumbing inspection, water tank cleaning, and other preventive tasks based on season, service history, and home profile.
9. **Predictive maintenance:** Over time, FixNest uses service history and customer patterns to recommend preventive actions before issues become emergencies.
10. **Annual maintenance plan:** Customer can convert recurring needs into a structured annual maintenance contract with planned visits, priority support, and bundled services.
11. **Home ecosystem expansion:** Customer can buy related products, request installation, access home improvement services, manage warranties, and maintain a complete home service history in one place.

### Future-state customer value

- Less uncertainty before booking.
- Faster diagnosis and routing.
- Better pricing confidence.
- Reduced emergency breakdowns through preventive reminders.
- Stronger accountability through service history, invoices, reviews, and support records.
- A single trusted platform for managing, repairing, upgrading, protecting, and improving the home.
