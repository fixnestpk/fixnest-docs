## Why FixNest Exists

FixNest exists because home maintenance in Pakistan is still too dependent on luck, scattered contacts, unclear prices, and informal promises. A homeowner should not need to gamble with trust every time an electrical issue, plumbing leak, or AC problem appears.

We are building FixNest to make home ownership safer, easier, faster, and more reliable through verified professionals, transparent operations, strong customer support, and eventually a complete home ecosystem.

## What We Believe

- Trust is the product before software is the product.
- Operational discipline creates better customer experiences than slogans.
- Customers deserve clarity before, during, and after every service.
- Technicians deserve dignity, structured work, fair expectations, and reputation-building opportunities.
- A great company is built through documented decisions, repeatable systems, and uncompromising standards.
- Technology should simplify real-world service delivery, not hide operational weakness.

## Problems We Are Solving in Pakistan

FixNest is designed to address practical problems that affect households across Pakistan:

- Unverified and inconsistent technician quality.
- Unclear pricing and unexpected charges.
- Weak accountability after service completion.
- Fragmented service history across phone calls, chats, and paper receipts.
- Lack of standardized complaint handling and escalation.
- Limited preventive maintenance guidance for homeowners.
- No single trusted destination for home services, home products, and home-care knowledge.

## The Company We Will Never Become

FixNest will never become:

- A company that treats trust as a marketing line instead of an operating system.
- A marketplace that ignores customer safety for faster growth.
- A platform that hides pricing, policies, or service limitations.
- A team that lets important decisions live only in chats, memory, or scattered files.
- A business that exploits technicians or customers for short-term margins.
- A brand that scales faster than its quality controls.

## Principles We Will Not Compromise

1. **Trust First** — every product, policy, and partnership must increase customer trust.
2. **Documented Decisions** — if a decision matters, it belongs in this repository.
3. **Operational Accountability** — every service should have an owner, status, record, and follow-up path.
4. **Transparent Expectations** — customers and technicians should know what is included, excluded, priced, and guaranteed.
5. **Quality Over Vanity Growth** — expansion is only successful when service quality can be maintained.
6. **Respect for the Home** — every technician, employee, and partner must treat the customer's home with care.
7. **Learning Culture** — complaints, failed jobs, and delays are signals to improve the system.

## How Every Employee Should Think

Every FixNest employee should ask:

- Does this decision make the customer safer or more confident?
- Is this process clear enough for a new teammate to follow?
- Are we documenting what future founders, operators, engineers, and investors will need to understand?
- Are we solving the root cause or only managing the symptom?
- Would we be proud if this policy became public?

## Relationship to Other Documents

This manifesto is the cultural foundation for FixNest. The business blueprint, business requirements document, product requirements, operations manual, and future technical architecture should all be consistent with the principles defined here.
