## 1. Purpose

This Business Requirements Document defines the foundation for FixNest as a trusted Home Ecosystem platform. It explains what FixNest is, who it serves, what problems it solves, what features are required, and how the business should evolve across launch, growth, and platform phases.

## 2. Business Objective

Build Pakistan's most trusted Home Ecosystem where homeowners can access verified professionals, essential home services, reliable customer support, transparent pricing, and home-related products through one platform.

## 3. Target Users

### 3.1 Homeowners and Tenants

People who need reliable help for home repair, maintenance, installation, emergency issues, inspections, and future home product purchases.

### 3.2 Technicians and Service Professionals

Electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, and future service providers who want structured jobs, reliable customer access, profile reputation, and consistent earning opportunities.

### 3.3 FixNest Operations Team

Internal staff responsible for booking management, technician assignment, customer support, quality assurance, complaints, pricing updates, and operational reporting.

### 3.4 Product Suppliers

Future suppliers of home-related products such as lights, switches, pipes, bathroom accessories, CCTV cameras, AC parts, solar panels, furniture, tools, kitchen accessories, and home decor.

## 4. Customer Problems

- Customers do not know which technician to trust.
- Customers must search randomly across contacts, social media, and local markets.
- Pricing is unclear and inconsistent.
- Workers may not arrive on time.
- Quality is unpredictable.
- There is no centralized service history.
- There is limited accountability if work fails after completion.
- There is no standard complaint or escalation process.
- Customers lack guidance on preventive maintenance and money-saving decisions.

## 5. Business Requirements

### 5.1 Trust Requirements

- Maintain verified worker profiles.
- Store worker skills, experience, certifications, and service areas.
- Collect customer ratings and reviews.
- Track completed job history for each professional.
- Define service guarantee rules for eligible services.

### 5.2 Booking Requirements

- Customers must be able to request a service.
- Customers must provide service type, location, preferred time, issue description, and contact information.
- FixNest must assign a suitable technician based on skill, location, availability, rating, and workload.
- Customers should receive booking confirmation and job updates.

### 5.3 Pricing Requirements

- Provide transparent pricing guidance for standard services.
- Allow custom quotation for non-standard work.
- Maintain a cost estimator in future phases.
- Provide digital invoices and payment records.

### 5.4 Customer Profile Requirements

- Store customer name, phone number, addresses, service history, invoices, complaints, reviews, and preferences.
- Support maintenance reminders in future phases.
- Support annual maintenance plans in future phases.

### 5.5 Worker Profile Requirements

- Store name, phone number, CNIC or identity information where legally appropriate, skill categories, experience, service areas, documents, certifications, ratings, work history, and availability.
- Support performance scoring and quality monitoring.

### 5.6 Operations Requirements

- Track each job from request to completion.
- Maintain status stages such as requested, confirmed, assigned, in progress, completed, cancelled, and disputed.
- Provide complaint handling and escalation workflows.
- Maintain quality control checks.
- Support emergency and disaster management workflows in future phases.

### 5.7 Content Requirements

- Publish articles, tips, maintenance guides, buying guides, energy-saving advice, and safety checklists.
- Use content to build trust, educate homeowners, and reduce avoidable service issues.

### 5.8 AI and Technology Requirements

- Design data and workflows to support future AI compatibility.
- Future AI features may include instant customer responses, service triage, cost estimation, technician matching, predictive maintenance, smart reminders, and WhatsApp-based assistance.

## 6. Phase Roadmap

### Phase 1: Launch

Primary focus:

- Electrician.
- Plumber.
- AC service.
- Manual or semi-manual booking workflow.
- Verified technician onboarding.
- Basic customer support.
- Basic pricing list.
- Service history tracking.

### Phase 2: Growth

Add:

- CCTV.
- Furniture repair.
- Carpenter.
- Painter.
- Solar.
- Cleaning.
- Water tank cleaning.
- Pest control.
- Digital quotation and invoice process.
- Ratings and reviews.
- Basic content library.
- Quality control reporting.

### Phase 3: Complete Platform

Add:

- Product marketplace.
- Annual Maintenance Contracts.
- Emergency services.
- Home inspection.
- Home renovation.
- Used home items exchange.
- AI assistant.
- Cost estimator.
- Predictive maintenance.
- Supplier partnerships.

## 7. Business Rules

- Trust and customer safety must be prioritized over short-term job volume.
- A technician should not be assigned to work outside verified skill categories.
- Every job should have a traceable record.
- Every complaint should have an owner, status, and resolution note.
- Pricing should be communicated clearly before work begins whenever possible.
- Customer data and worker data should be handled carefully and only used for business purposes.
- New features should support at least one brand promise: trust, convenience, transparency, quality, or innovation.

## 8. Success Metrics

- Number of completed jobs.
- Repeat customer rate.
- Customer satisfaction score.
- Average response time.
- Average job completion time.
- Complaint rate.
- Technician reliability score.
- Revenue per service category.
- Customer acquisition cost.
- Number of verified technicians.
- Number of active service areas.

## 9. Key Risks

- Poor technician quality damaging brand trust.
- Inconsistent pricing causing customer dissatisfaction.
- Slow response times reducing reliability.
- Weak complaint handling.
- Insufficient technician supply during peak demand.
- Safety issues during home visits.
- Operational complexity during expansion.

## 10. Mitigation Strategy

- Start with fewer services and strong controls.
- Verify technicians before onboarding.
- Use clear SOPs and job tracking.
- Create complaint and escalation rules from day one.
- Monitor reviews and repeat complaints.
- Build pricing guidance before scaling.
- Expand service categories only when operations can support quality.
