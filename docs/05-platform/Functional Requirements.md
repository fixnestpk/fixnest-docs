# FixNest Website Requirements Document v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the requirements for the FixNest website. The website should introduce the brand, generate service bookings, build trust, educate homeowners, and create a foundation for future platform features.

## 2. Website Objectives

The website should:

- Explain FixNest clearly.
- Convert visitors into service bookings.
- Build trust through verified professionals, transparent pricing, and customer support.
- Present launch services: electrician, plumber, and AC service.
- Support content marketing through guides and articles.
- Prepare for future customer accounts, technician profiles, marketplace, and AI assistant features.

## 3. Target Users

- Homeowners and tenants who need home repairs.
- Technicians who want to join the platform.
- Partners and suppliers.
- Investors or business stakeholders.
- Internal operations staff using early forms or admin workflows.

## 4. Core Pages

### 4.1 Home Page

Must include:

- Clear headline and value proposition.
- Primary call to action to book a service.
- Service categories.
- Trust indicators.
- How it works.
- Customer benefits.
- Testimonials when available.
- Service area information.

### 4.2 Services Page

Must list active services and explain what is included, pricing guidance, process, warranty conditions, and booking options.

### 4.3 Service Detail Pages

Recommended pages:

- Electrician services.
- Plumbing services.
- AC services.

Each page should include common problems, service scope, what is excluded, pricing guidance, FAQs, and booking CTA.

### 4.4 Book a Service Page

Must collect:

- Name.
- Phone number.
- Address or area.
- Service category.
- Issue description.
- Preferred date and time.
- Photos or videos where supported.
- Consent to be contacted.

### 4.5 About Page

Should explain FixNest vision, mission, trust model, and commitment to verified professionals.

### 4.6 Technician Signup Page

Must collect technician name, phone, city, service categories, experience, service areas, documents or verification readiness, and consent for onboarding contact.

### 4.7 Blog or Guides Page

Should host maintenance guides, safety tips, seasonal checklists, buying guides, and cost-saving advice.

### 4.8 Contact Page

Must include phone, WhatsApp, email or contact form, support hours, service areas, and escalation instructions.

### 4.9 Legal Pages

Recommended legal pages:

- Terms and conditions.
- Privacy policy.
- Refund and cancellation policy.
- Service warranty policy.

## 5. Functional Requirements

- Users can submit a service booking request.
- Users can request technician onboarding.
- Users can contact support.
- Admin or operations team receives booking details reliably.
- Website captures lead source for marketing attribution.
- Website supports analytics tracking.
- Website is mobile responsive.
- Website loads quickly on common mobile networks.
- Website supports SEO-friendly URLs and metadata.
- Website supports future customer login and service history features.

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

- Mobile-first design.
- Simple navigation.
- Fast page load.
- Secure form submission.
- Spam protection.
- Accessible contrast and readable typography.
- Clear error messages.
- Backup or export path for submitted leads.

## 7. Trust Requirements

The website should communicate:

- Verified technicians.
- Standardized service process.
- Transparent pricing guidance.
- Customer support availability.
- Complaint and escalation process.
- Digital invoice or service record.
- Ratings and reviews when available.

## 8. SEO Requirements

Initial SEO focus should include:

- Electrician services in target cities.
- Plumber services in target cities.
- AC service in target cities.
- Home maintenance tips in Pakistan.
- Seasonal AC maintenance.
- Leakage repair guidance.
- Electrical safety content.

Each service page should have a unique title, description, heading structure, internal links, FAQs, and local service-area references.

## 9. Content Requirements

Website tone should be trustworthy, simple, helpful, and professional. Avoid overpromising. Content should explain what FixNest does, how pricing works, how technicians are verified, and how customers can get help if something goes wrong.

## 10. Analytics Requirements

Track:

- Page views.
- Booking form starts.
- Booking form submissions.
- WhatsApp clicks.
- Phone clicks.
- Technician signup submissions.
- Blog visits.
- Conversion by channel.
- Conversion by service category.

## 11. Future Requirements

Future website phases may include:

- Customer accounts.
- Service history.
- Digital invoices.
- Technician profiles.
- Reviews.
- Cost estimator.
- AI troubleshooting assistant.
- Product marketplace.
- Annual Maintenance Contract checkout.
- Online payments.

## 12. Acceptance Criteria

The launch website is acceptable when:

- Customers can understand FixNest within five seconds of landing on the home page.
- Customers can book a service on mobile without confusion.
- Operations receives all booking submissions.
- Active services are clearly described.
- Contact and support information are visible.
- Basic analytics are installed.
- Legal and policy pages are available or scheduled before paid campaigns begin.
