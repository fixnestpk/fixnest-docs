## Brand Positioning

FixNest is Pakistan's trusted Home Ecosystem for verified home services, reliable support, transparent processes, and future home-related products.

## Brand Promise

Verified professionals. Transparent service. One trusted platform for your home.

## Brand Attributes

- Trustworthy.
- Professional.
- Helpful.
- Transparent.
- Modern.
- Accountable.
- Calm under pressure.

## Voice and Tone

FixNest should sound clear, respectful, practical, reassuring, and professional. Communications should avoid exaggerated promises and focus on trust, service quality, and customer confidence.

### Voice Rules

- Use simple language that customers can understand quickly.
- Be specific about service windows, fees, and next steps.
- Avoid technical jargon unless it is explained.
- Do not overpromise exact repair outcomes before inspection.
- Acknowledge inconvenience when customers report urgent or failed service.

### Example Tone

| Scenario | Preferred Tone | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Clear and reassuring | Vague or overly casual |
| Technician delay | Apologetic and action-oriented | Defensive language |
| Price explanation | Transparent and factual | Pressure or ambiguity |
| Complaint response | Responsible and empathetic | Blame shifting |

## Messaging Pillars

### Trust

FixNest verifies technicians, tracks jobs, and gives customers a support path.

### Convenience

Customers can request help through a guided process instead of searching randomly.

### Transparency

Pricing guidance, inspection fees, approvals, and invoices should be clearly communicated.

### Care for the Home

FixNest helps customers maintain the home over time, not only during emergencies.

## Tagline Options

- Verified Help for Every Home.
- Home Services You Can Trust.
- One Platform for a Better Home.

## Visual Direction

### Colors

Recommended starting palette:

- Trust Blue: primary brand color for reliability and technology.
- Nest Green: secondary color for home care, safety, and growth.
- Warm Neutral: background color for calm, household-friendly design.
- Alert Amber: warnings, urgent reminders, and service attention.
- Success Green: completed jobs, confirmations, and positive status.

Exact hex values should be finalized during visual identity design and stored in `docs/03-brand/Colors/` or an equivalent asset directory.

### Typography

Use readable, modern typography that works well in English and future Urdu or Roman Urdu contexts. Prioritize legibility on mobile screens, invoices, and WhatsApp graphics.

### Logo Guidance

Logo assets should communicate home, safety, reliability, and service. Final files should include light, dark, monochrome, square, horizontal, and favicon versions.

## Content Style

- Use sentence case for headings and UI labels unless a campaign intentionally requires title case.
- Prefer active voice.
- Use customer-first language: "Your technician is assigned" instead of "Technician assigned by ops".
- Confirm amounts, times, and names in structured formats.
