# FixNest Brand Guide

## Brand Positioning

FixNest is Pakistan's trusted Home Ecosystem for verified home services, reliable support, transparent processes, and future home-related products.

## Brand Attributes

- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Helpful
- Transparent
- Modern
- Accountable

## Voice and Tone

FixNest should sound clear, respectful, practical, reassuring, and professional. Communications should avoid exaggerated promises and focus on trust, service quality, and customer confidence.

## Core Message

Verified professionals. Transparent service. One trusted platform for your home.
