# FixNest

> Pakistan's Trusted Home Ecosystem

## Overview

FixNest is building a unified Home Ecosystem that connects homeowners with trusted professionals, home-related products, intelligent technology, and exceptional customer support through a single platform.

Our vision is to make home ownership safer, easier, faster, and more reliable.

---

## Vision

To become Pakistan's most trusted Home Ecosystem by providing every essential home service and home-related product through one trusted platform.

---

## Mission

To simplify home maintenance through verified professionals, transparent pricing, standardized workflows, and technology-driven customer experiences.
