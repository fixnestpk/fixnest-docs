> Pakistan's Trusted Home Ecosystem

## Overview

FixNest Docs is the dedicated documentation repository for FixNest. It is the single source of truth for company philosophy, business decisions, research, brand, operations, product, technology, marketing, finance, legal, and roadmap planning.

FixNest is building a unified Home Ecosystem that connects homeowners with trusted professionals, home-related products, intelligent technology, and exceptional customer support through a single platform.

Our vision is to make home ownership safer, easier, faster, and more reliable.

## Single Source of Truth

Every important FixNest decision should be documented in this repository. Decisions made through WhatsApp, calls, meetings, emails, or external files are not official until they are captured here.

This repository should be maintained as if another founder joins next year, a team of 20 engineers starts development, an investor requests due diligence, or FixNest raises funding.

## Documentation Structure

FixNest documentation is organized using numbered sections so the knowledge base remains intuitive as it grows.

- `docs/00-overview/` — company-level foundations, manifesto, documentation standards, and onboarding context.
- `docs/01-business/` — vision, mission, value proposition, business blueprint, and business model canvas.
- `docs/02-research/` — market research, competitor analysis, personas, and customer journey research.
- `docs/03-brand/` — brand guide, logo assets, and color guidance.
- `docs/04-operations/` — operations manual, service SOPs, technician SOP, and customer SOP.
- `docs/05-product/` — BRD, future PRD, functional requirements, features, and user stories.
- `docs/06-technology/` — technology strategy, future architecture, APIs, database, infrastructure, and coding standards.
- `docs/07-marketing/` — marketing strategy, content strategy, launch strategy, and growth documents.
- `docs/08-finance/` — pricing strategy, financial plan, forecasts, and unit economics.
- `docs/09-legal/` — legal planning, compliance, policy, privacy, risk, and contract documents.
- `docs/10-roadmap/` — documentation and development roadmap materials.
- `assets/` — reusable brand, document, and marketing assets.
- `diagrams/` — architecture, service, process, and journey diagrams.
